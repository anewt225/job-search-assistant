# Resume & Cover Letter Templates (fallback when the user has no format of their own)

## Resume (ATS-safe, 1–2 pages)
Single column. No tables, text boxes, icons or headers/footers carrying content. Use standard section
names. Dates right-aligned, or after the title as `Mon YYYY – Mon YYYY`.

```
FIRST LAST
City, ST · email · phone · linkedin.com/in/handle · portfolio (optional)

TARGET TITLE | 2–3 KEYWORD PILLARS            ← mirrors the JD title; in the top third
2–3 line summary: who you are for this role, your signature result, your differentiator.

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
Company — City, ST (one-line descriptor if the company isn't well known)
Title                                                     Mon YYYY – Present
• Verb + what + how + result with a Number/Name/Notable (≤2 lines)
• 3–6 bullets for recent roles, 1–3 for older ones; order by relevance to the JD

EDUCATION
Degree, Field — School                                   YYYY (optional after ~10 yrs)

CERTIFICATIONS · SKILLS
Certifications: …        Skills: tools, methods, domains (use JD wording)
```

## Bullet patterns (GLORY + 3 Ns)
- Process: Redesigned [process], cutting [metric] by [X]% and saving [hours/$] per [period] for [N people].
- Build: Built [tool/system/framework] to automate [manual work], reducing turnaround from [X] to [Y].
- Lead: Led a [N]-team initiative with [named partner/function] to [outcome], delivering [result].
- Analyse: Analysed [N options/markets] and recommended [decision] to [named leaders], informing a [$X] decision.
- Customer: Improved [CSAT/NPS/retention] from [X] to [Y] by [action].
**Resume tiers** (JC): Basic = error-free responsibilities · Good = major projects with results where
they're quantifiable · Great = impact and root causes, results every time.

## Cover letter (Pareto: 80% reusable base, 20% custom; 250–350 words)
```
Dear [Hiring Manager name, or "Hiring Team"],

[HOOK, the custom part] [Company]'s [recent initiative/launch/news] caught my attention because [specific reason tied to my experience]. As someone who has [signature accomplishment], I'm excited about the chance to [specific contribution] as your [Role].
  — or, with a referral — [Name] suggested I reach out about the [Role]; after learning about [team/initiative], I'm confident my experience in [area] fits.

[PROOF 1] At [Company], I [CAR in 2–3 sentences, with a number].
[PROOF 2] I also [second relevant result, mapped to a different JD requirement].

[WHY YOU, the custom part] What draws me to [Company] is [authentic, specific, informed reason].

I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I can help [team] [goal]. Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Name]
```
Check: competence (the proof) **and** warmth (genuine interest) · the top keywords used naturally · no
paragraph that restates the resume line by line.

## Starter-resume interview (no resume on file, ~10 min)
Ask, one at a time: target role → last 3 roles (company, title, dates) → for each, 3 things you're proud
of, with numbers if you have them → tools you use weekly → education and certifications → anything
unusual that sets you apart. Then build the resume from the template above and mark every guessed metric
`[X?]`.
