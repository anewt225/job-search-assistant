# Discovery Quiz Bank

Draw from every round and shuffle the order. Rephrase questions freely and swap in the user's own world
(industry, past roles) to keep them fresh. **Quick mode**: 3 from each round. **Full mode**: 6–8 from each
round, plus 1–2 exercises from `exercises.md`.

## Round 1: Energy (what you love and hate). Sources: Terry (loves/hates), DYL (Good Time Journal)
1. *This or that:* untangling a mess **or** building something from scratch?
2. *Scenario:* It's 4pm on a great workday and you're still energised. What did you spend the afternoon on? (a) persuading a room (b) deep in data or a model (c) coaching someone through a problem (d) shipping something tangible (e) negotiating a deal (f) other
3. *Rank 3:* people · problems · craft · mission · money · autonomy · status · learning
4. *Open (1 line):* Which task did you last lose track of time doing?
5. *Scenario:* Your calendar is fully booked for a week with one kind of meeting. Which week would you dread? (a) status updates (b) customer calls (c) 1:1 coaching (d) brainstorms (e) budget reviews
6. *This or that:* being the expert in the room **or** the person connecting the experts?
7. *Open:* Which past job task would you pay to never do again?
8. *Multiple choice:* At a party you end up (a) hosting (b) in a deep 1:1 (c) fixing the playlist or logistics (d) debating ideas (e) leaving early, happily

## Round 2: Strengths & skills. Sources: Parachute (transferable skills), Job Closer (You Bet Your Life), Mann
1. *You Bet Your Life:* You have 60 seconds. Name ONE thing you might be in the top 1% at. (Accept anything, then probe it.)
2. *Multiple choice:* Friends call you when they need (a) a plan (b) a pep talk (c) a spreadsheet (d) an honest opinion (e) an intro to someone (f) something fixed
3. *Story prompt:* Tell me about a time you were proud of a result. Two sentences. (Then extract the verbs: *analysed, persuaded, organised, built, taught…*)
4. *Parachute skills sort:* Of **data · people · things · ideas**, which do you work with best? And which drains you?
5. *This or that:* starting things **or** finishing things?
6. *Scenario:* A project is 2 weeks late. Your instinct: (a) re-plan the timeline (b) rally the team (c) dig into the root cause (d) escalate and reset expectations (e) roll up your sleeves and do it
7. *Open:* What compliment do you get that you secretly agree with?
8. *Pick 2:* strategic · analytical · creative · relational · operational · technical · persuasive · nurturing

## Round 3: Conditions & values (must-haves and must-nots). Sources: Terry, Parachute (Flower), Linked (learning/earning/yearning)
1. *Must-nots, fast:* Name 3 things that would make you quit within 6 months.
2. *Rank:* People (who you work with) · Growth (learning) · Purpose (impact) · Pay · Flexibility · Stability
3. *Multiple choice:* Ideal team size: (a) solo (b) 2–5 (c) 6–15 (d) big org, many teams
4. *This or that:* startup chaos **or** big-company structure?
5. *Scenario:* Two offers. A pays 15% more; B has a manager you'd learn a ton from. Which, and why?
6. *Multiple choice:* Where do you do your best work? (a) remote (b) hybrid (c) in person (d) on the move or in the field
7. *Salary floor (optional):* What's the number below which you'd say no? And the "happy" number?
8. *Open:* Which industries or problems make you lean in when they come up in the news?
9. *This or that:* customer-facing **or** behind the scenes?

## Round 4: Direction (roles, market, pivot size). Sources: Pivot, DYL (Odyssey Plans), Mann (Well-th), Dalton (LAMP)
1. *Brain dump:* List every job you'd enjoy *and* could plausibly get. No filtering, 2 minutes.
2. *Odyssey, quick:* Describe 3 five-year futures in one line each: (1) the current path (2) if path 1 vanished (3) if money and image didn't matter.
3. *Pivot size:* What would change in your next move? Industry · function · level · location. (Changing more than 1–2 at once makes the move harder for the market to believe.)
4. *Multiple choice:* What do you want the next role to *do for you*? (a) a step up in title or pay (b) learning a new domain (c) stability (d) meaning (e) a bridge to something else
5. *Open:* Name 3 companies or orgs you'd be proud to work for, and what they have in common.
6. *Scenario:* A recruiter says, "I can place you tomorrow as ___." What do you hope fills that blank? What do you fear does?
7. *Open:* Who has a job you'd want in 5 years? (Real people. These become prototype conversations.)

## Scoring (silent tally → synthesis)
| Signal | Points toward |
|---|---|
| Untangling, root cause, re-plan, data | Operations · analytics · strategy · program management |
| Persuading, negotiating, hosting | Sales · BD · partnerships · client-facing consulting |
| Coaching, pep talks, 1:1s | People management · enablement · L&D · customer success |
| Building from scratch, shipping, starting | Product · founding roles · new-program launches |
| Expert in the room, deep work, things | Specialist / IC tracks (engineering, analytics, design) |
| Connecting experts, relational | Program / partnership / community roles |
| Ideas, creative, debating | Strategy · marketing · content · research |
Treat these as hypotheses, not verdicts. Weight the user's corrections and real evidence (proud results,
compliments they agree with) above multiple-choice picks.
