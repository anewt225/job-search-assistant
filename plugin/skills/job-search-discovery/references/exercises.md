# Discovery Exercises (one at a time, on request or in Full mode)

| Exercise | Source | How to run it in chat (≤10 min) | Produces |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Flower** | Bolles, *What Color Is Your Parachute?* | 7 petals, 2–3 answers each: favourite knowledge/fields · people you like working with · transferable skills (top 5) · working conditions · salary and level · place (geography) · purpose/values. Ask one petal per turn | A one-page Flower summary to pass to role scoring |
| **Prioritizing Grid** | Bolles | Pairwise "which matters more?" across 6–10 items (skills or values). Interactive tool: https://anewt225.github.io/parachute/prioritizing-grid/ | A ranked list |
| **Loves / Hates → Must-Haves / Must-Nots** | Terry, *Never Search Alone* (Mnookin Two-Pager) | Brain dump of hates (jobs, tasks, cultures) → loves → convert each into a must-not or must-have. "Draft, not perfect." | Two-pager §1–3 |
| **Workview + Lifeview** | Burnett & Evans, *Designing Your Life* | ~150 words each: what is work for? what gives life meaning? Then: where do they agree or clash? | Coherence check for role choices |
| **Odyssey Plans** | DYL | 3 five-year plans: current path · if path 1 vanished · wild card. Rate each on resources, liking, confidence and coherence (0–4) | Lanes for the target-role list |
| **Good Time Journal (mini)** | DYL | Recall the last 2 weeks of activities. Rate engagement and energy (high/low) for each, then spot patterns | Energizers / drainers |
| **HWPL dashboard** | DYL | Health · Work · Play · Love, each 0–4. Is now the time for a big pivot or a small one? | Pivot-size sanity check |
| **You Bet Your Life + Brain Dump** | Dalton, *The Job Closer* | One top-1% skill in 60 seconds, then list every enjoyable and feasible job | Raw role list |
| **Well-th Formula** | Mann, *Reverse the Search* | Score each candidate role on Values + Strengths + Market Demand (1–5 each) | Ranked roles |
| **One-degree pivot** | Blake, *Pivot* | For each candidate role, count what changes (industry, function, level, location). Prefer 1–2 changes. Plant (strengths) → Scan (research) → Pilot (small experiments) | Credibility check |
| **Learning / Earning / Yearning** | Garriott & Schifeling, *Linked* | Score roles on People, Growth and Purpose fit | Tiebreaker |
| **Prototype conversations** | DYL + Terry (listening tour) | Name 3 people doing the candidate role, then hand off to the networking module for outreach | Validation plan |
