# **Chapter 5: Interview with Confidence**

***"33% of interviewers admit to making up their minds within the first 90 seconds of an interview."***

## **Two Things Interviewers Evaluate**

1. **Competence** \- Can they do the job?  
2. **Warmth** \- Do I want to do the job with them?

## **CAR Method**

| Element | Purpose |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Challenge** | Set up the problem or situation |
| **Action** | Describe what you specifically did |
| **Result** | Quantify the outcome |

| 💬 Prompt 25: Generate Interview Answers *Generate a list of the 10 most likely interview questions I'll face based on the following job description. And for each question, generate an answer in Challenge \- Action \- Result format, drawing only from the following resume. Job description: PASTE JOB DESCRIPTION Resume: PASTE RESUME* |
| :---- |

## **Practice**

| 💬 Prompt 26: Practice Interview *I want you to interview me for an X ROLE at Y COMPANY. Start by asking me questions based on the job description below. And then, after I answer, please provide constructive criticism on my answer \- and ask the next question. Here is the job description: JOB DESCRIPTION* |
| :---- |

*LinkedIn Interview Prep: linkedin.com/interview-prep*

### **Warmth Checklist**

* Were answers under 2 minutes? (No one likes a long talker)  
* Speaking pace: 130-160 words per minute  
* Vary pitch to maintain engagement  
* Avoid filler words (um, like, etc.)

## **Evaluate Offers**

| :---- |

## **Background Check Your Boss**

Steps: 1\) Set target (your prospective boss), 2\) Find former employees on LinkedIn (Past Company filter), 3\) Filter for your prospective team, 4\) Find common ground, 5\) Reach out for feedback  
*"Connection Request: Did you work with X BOSS at Y COMPANY by any chance? If so, I'd love to know if you'd recommend taking a role on their team since I've just gotten an offer from them."*

## **Auction Dynamics**

Three keys to negotiation leverage:

3. Have as many bidders as possible  
4. Make the bidders aware of each other  
5. Understand the bidders' motivations

| 💬 Prompt 30: Signal Competition *Generate a short, conversational email to the recruiter at X COMPANY, letting them know about my other interviews/offers at Y COMPANY and Z COMPANY. Ask them if, in light of these competitive opportunities, they can do ABC (accelerate their interview process, make a final decision sooner, or offer a more competitive package).* |
| :---- |

## **Set Your Anchor**

Find the best comparison: current salary, other offers, or market rate (Glassdoor, Payscale)

| 💬 Prompt 31: Initial Negotiation *Generate a short, conversational email to my recruiter, thanking them for their kind job offer (INSERT OFFER), and letting them know that I'd like to explore what's possible based on the following facts: \-Current Salary \-Other Offers \-Pay Research for Role/Location* |
| :---- |

| 💬 Prompt 32: Counter Offer *I asked my recruiter to match this offer: X. They came back with the following message: Y. Generate a short, conversational response that asks them to do better, given my opportunities at these other firms: Z* |
| :---- |

## **Non-Salary Alternatives**

* Sign-on bonus  
* Equity  
* Relocation package  
* Better title  
* Remote work flexibility  
* More vacation time  
* Earlier or later start date

| 💬 Prompt 33: Trade-off Offer *Generate a short, conversational email to my recruiter offering a concession of X salary in exchange for Y benefit.* |
| :---- |

| 💬 Prompt 34: Final Push *Generate a short, conversational email to my recruiter letting them know that I'm willing to consider signing their offer today and turning down my other offers \- but only if they can agree to X compensation now.* |
| :---- |
