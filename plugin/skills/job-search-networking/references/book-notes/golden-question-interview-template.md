## Agenda for the conversation

1. **Context on yourself.**  
   Provide context about yourself. Say a few words about your career and how you are doing research to understand where there might be job opportunities for someone like you.  
      
2. **Ask the Golden Question.**  
   Then ask: “If you were in my shoes, how would you approach this job search?” And resist the temptation to say a lot after asking this. Be quiet. Listen. Let them think and reflect.  
      
3. **Share your Mnookin Two-Pager (**Link to your Mnookin Two-Pager: \_\_)  
   At the end of the meeting, share your Mnookin Two-Pager, and ask the same questions I advised you to ask at the end of your Reverse Exit Interviews.  
   1. What do you think of this Two-Pager? Do you think this a good description of me — accurate in terms of what I’m good at and enjoy — and what I don’t like? Is there anything missing? Is there anything going on in the market — any trends or new roles, etc. — that you think I would be a fit for?  
   2. What do you think of my short-term career goals?  
   3. Is there anyone else I should talk to?
