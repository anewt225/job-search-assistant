# Message Templates

Fill `[brackets]` from the profile and inputs. Keep Dalton brackets (`< >`, `<< >>`, `<<< >>>`) visible on
cold 6-Point drafts. Verbatim Never Search Alone originals: `book-notes/outreach-thank-you-and-follow-up-notes.md`.

## Cold — Dalton 6-Point email (default for strangers)
```
Subject: Your < shared experience phrase >

Hi [First name],

I'm [My name], << my connection to you >>. May I chat with you briefly about your < shared experience phrase >? I'm trying to learn more about <<< category of my interest >>>, so your insights would be greatly appreciated.

Best regards,
[My name]
```
No connection → delete the whole "I'm [name], << >>" sentence. Don't invent one.

*Worked example (Dalton):* "Subject: Your product management experience at Waymo / Hi Jeff, I'm Becca,
a fellow MBA (Duke '24). May I chat with you briefly about your product management experience at Waymo?
I am trying to learn more about product management in the autonomous vehicle space, so your insights
would be greatly appreciated. Best regards, Becca"

## LinkedIn connection note (≤300 chars)
`Hi [Name] — [specific thing about their work/post/session]. I'm exploring [category] and would value your perspective. [Real connection if any.] Thanks!`

## LinkedIn DM (already connected) — same 6-Point rules, drop the subject line.

## Warm reconnect / listening tour (Terry style, direct)
```
Hi [Name] — it's been a while. [Genuine observation about their recent move/news.]
I'm early in my next career move and doing a short "listening tour" before I target anything — not asking for leads. Would you be up for 20–30 min by phone or video in the next couple of weeks? I'd love your honest take on [what I did well / where you'd see me fitting / how the market looks from where you sit].
[Scheduling link or 2–3 time options]
```
Text/Slack version: 3 sentences max, same ask.

## Recruiter advice ask (Terry)
Ask for 20–30 min; frame as "how the market sees me". Three questions: what could you place me in
tomorrow with no sweat · what would be a stretch · what's out of reach? Include LinkedIn URL.

## Referral ask (Linked) — only after a prior conversation, or when offered
```
Subject: [Role] at [Company] — referral?

Hi [Name], thanks again for [specific thing from our chat]. A [Role] opening just posted ([link]) that lines up with [1-line why]. Would you be comfortable referring me? Resume attached — happy to send a 3-line blurb you can paste. And if there's a referral bonus, I hope it comes your way!
```

## Intro request (to a connector)
Ask for the intro + include a **forwardable blurb** (3 sentences: who you are, why this person, the 20-min
ask) so the connector does zero writing.

## Follow-up — no reply
After ~3 business days, try a second contact at the same employer. After ~7, one bump in the same thread:
`Hi [Name] — floating this back up in case it got buried. Still keen to hear your take on < x > if you have 15 minutes in the next few weeks.`
Then stop. Silence is data, not a prompt to rewrite.

## Thank-you (within 24h of a conversation)
```
Hi [Name], thank you for the time today. Your point about [specific A] — and [specific B] — already changed how I'm approaching [X]. I'll [concrete next step you'll take] and keep you posted. If I can ever return the favour — [specific offer, e.g. an intro, a resource] — just say the word.
```

## Update note (monthly–quarterly, to everyone you've spoken to)
```
Subject: Quick update on my search

Hi [Name], a short update since we spoke: [1–2 concrete moves — applied your advice on X, met Y, narrowed to Z].
Your advice on [thing] shaped [result]. Right now I'm focused on [category]; if anyone comes to mind who'd be good to talk to, I'd be grateful — no pressure at all.
Thanks again, [Name]
```
Terry's observation: update notes are where unprompted introductions come from.

## Event follow-up (within 24–48h)
`Hi [Name], great meeting you at [event] — I enjoyed [specific thing they said]. [Promised item / resource / intro]. Would you be open to a 20-min call in the next few weeks to continue the conversation about [topic]?`
