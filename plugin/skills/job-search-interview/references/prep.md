# Interview Prep

**Inputs** (taken from the found interview, or asked for in one message): company, role/JD, stage,
interviewer(s), date/time, format (video, phone or on-site), and anything the recruiter said.

## Steps
1. **Context pull.** JD (from the link, the Opportunity, or an email), resume (profile §6), prior meetings with
   this company (data store), and the email thread with the recruiter. Read only what you need.
2. **Quick research.** Use 3–5 searches at most: company news from the last 6 months, strategy and
   competitors, the interviewer's role, background and any posts or talks, and the interview process if
   it's public. Skip anything already in the data store.
3. **Fit map.** List the top 6–8 JD requirements and the user's evidence for each (strong, partial or gap).
   Gaps get a bridging story or an honest framing, never a fabrication.
4. **Build the brief** using the template below, scaled to the stage. A recruiter screen is one page.
   A final round is fuller.
5. **Offer outputs** in one message, multi-select:
   a) save the brief file · b) prep tasks in the task tool · c) a prep calendar block (default 60 min, the
   day before) plus a 10-min "glance" block just before the interview · d) update the Meeting or Opportunity
   record · e) start a mock now (Practice mode)
6. **Prep tasks** are flat, with due dates working back from the interview: research done (−3d) · CAR
   stories drafted (−2d) · mock run (−1d) · questions finalised (−1d) · logistics checked: link, outfit,
   setup (−1d) · thank-you drafted (+0d, in the evening).

## Brief template
```
# <Stage> — <Role> — <Company>   (<date, time, TZ> · <format> · <interviewer>)

## 30-second version
Why they're hiring · what they're probably worried about · the 3 things I must land

## Company & role
What they do, recent news (with dates), strategy and pain points (INFERRED where it applies)

## Interviewer
Role, background, a hook for small talk, what they likely care about

## Fit map
| JD requirement | My evidence | Strength | Story to use |

## My answers
- TMAY (FIT): 60–90 sec script plus a 30-sec version
- Why <Company> / why this role (RAC)
- 5–8 likely questions → CAR outline each (Challenge · Action · Result + one N)
- A weakness or failure → SCAR outline
- Gaps → bridging framing

## Questions I'll ask (pick 3–5)
TIARA plus consultative questions tailored to this team, ending with next steps and timing

## Logistics & reminders
Link or location · who to ask for · comp stance for this stage · energy note
```
