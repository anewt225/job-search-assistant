FAQ

Which AI apps does it work in?

Any app that reads Agent Skills, the open SKILL.md format: Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Claude Code, and others such as Cursor and GitHub Copilot. The skills never assume which app they're in. App-specific steps live in one table (hosts.md).

What does it cost?

The skills are free. They run on your existing AI plan (Claude Pro or higher; a ChatGPT workspace with Skills turned on; or Codex). Connectors and big imports use more of your usage. The Notion template is the cheapest way to set up.

Will it send emails for me?

No. It only drafts. It never sends messages and never guesses email addresses.

Do I need any connectors?

No. Every module falls back to paste or upload. Connectors just save you copy-pasting.

Where is my data?

Only in your own AI account and your own tools. This page stores nothing on a server; your answers stay in your browser.
